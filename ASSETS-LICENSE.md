## Original visuals and 3D models

The game interface, illustrative graphics and the twenty-model isometric
industrial kit originate in the Casholoco project by Cédric Bernadac.
The kit is generated from editable Blender sources in `art_sources/isometric/`
and `tools/isometric/build_library.py`; no external model or texture is listed
in the kit documentation. Runtime model: `isometric/library.glb`.

## Music and short musical cues

The 37 audio files originate in the Casholoco project’s Suno production batch.
`docs/AUDIO_CREDITS.csv` on the source branch records every track, source URL,
model, production date, plan at creation and audio processing. It records
Suno v6 / Premier on 29 September 2026. This provenance is not itself a
verification of ownership or a blanket sublicense for remixes.

The publisher has authorized their inclusion and adaptation in these games and
